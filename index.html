OUR MISSION

Collaboration. Innovation. Open Source.

NST Student Developer Club is a vibrant community of tech enthusiasts, developers, and innovators at Newton School of Technology striving to learn, build, and share impactful projects.

We foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, and open-source contributions among students—preparing them to excel in real-world engineering challenges and make a mark in the tech industry.

"The best way to learn is by doing, and the best way to grow is by sharing."

WHAT WE DO

Our Core Activities

From open-source contributions to hackathon wins, we provide a platform for students to collaborate, learn, and grow together.

Open-Source Projects

Build and contribute to impactful projects that matter. From web apps to AI/ML tools — we ship code, write docs, fix bugs, and build tools the community depends on.

Workshops & Webinars

Learn the latest tech trends and tools through hands-on sessions led by experienced peers and industry mentors.

Competitions & Hackathons

Showcase your skills and solve real-world problems. We organize coding challenges and compete at external hackathons.

Mentorship Programs

Collaborate with experienced peers and mentors. Get 1-on-1 guidance to write quality code and grow as an engineer.

Knowledge Sharing

Encourage continuous learning and collaboration. Share insights, write blogs, and present tech talks to the community.
